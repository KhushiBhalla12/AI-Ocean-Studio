About the project

AI Ocean Studio — emotion-driven music discovery 🦪

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ❓

Is my camera or microphone recording saved?
No. 100% of video frame analysis and voice RMS processing happens client-side in real time. No video or audio is ever stored or uploaded.
How does voice detection complement facial detection?
When facial expressions are neutral or camera lighting is dim, microphone energy spectrum levels help differentiate between a relaxed state and fatigue.
